# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

UK shoppers arriving from high-intent search campaigns who want a fast, credible comparison of LED face masks before choosing a retailer.

## Product Purpose

TrustpilotReview's UK comparison page ranks major LED face masks, makes the evaluation easy to scan, and sends qualified visitors to the current Buudy UK product page. Success means clear comparison, immediate comprehension on mobile and desktop, reliable outbound navigation, and intact advertising attribution.

## Brand Personality

Decisive, evidence-led, accessible. The page should feel like a focused consumer comparison publication, not a generic ecommerce landing page or decorative beauty campaign.

## Anti-references

Avoid the current oversized cream editorial treatment, cramped card grids, weak hierarchy, hidden product context, excessive animation, broken media, encoding damage, and any layout that differs materially from the approved BestLedFaceMask UK reference.

## Design Principles

1. Use the approved BestLedFaceMask UK page as the visual source of truth.
2. Show comparison context and expert framing before the ranked products.
3. Keep Buudy calls to action immediate and visually dominant without delaying navigation.
4. Preserve natural image ratios and readable long-form line lengths.
5. Treat tracking, links, mobile layout, and rollback as production requirements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic headings and links, keyboard-accessible actions, readable contrast, responsive layouts, reduced-motion compatibility, and text that remains legible at browser zoom.
