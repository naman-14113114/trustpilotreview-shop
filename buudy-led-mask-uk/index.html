Before you buy

LED mask questions customers ask before checkout.

Clear answers reduce hesitation, especially for first-time LED buyers.

How quickly will I see results?

Most routines need consistency. Customers usually look for visible changes over 2 to 6 weeks, depending on skin concern, age, routine, and how often they use the mask.

Which light mode should I start with?

Start with red plus near-infrared for general glow, firmness, and fine-line concerns. Use blue for blemish-prone routines and yellow when the goal is a brighter-looking, more even tone.

Can I use it with retinol, AHA, or BHA?

Keep LED sessions gentle. Avoid using the mask directly over irritated skin, open wounds, or immediately after strong actives such as high-strength acids or retinoids.

Is it painful?

No. LED light therapy is non-invasive and should feel comfortable. If your skin feels hot, irritated, or unusual, stop using the device and let your skin recover.

Who should avoid LED masks?

Do not use over open wounds, active irritation, or on the eyelids. Ask a clinician first if you are pregnant, photosensitive, taking photosensitising medication, or managing a medical skin condition.
